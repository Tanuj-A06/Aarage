    What a bet paysArenaBattle.sol
    Back the winner
    Your stake back in full, plus your share of the losing pool. The principal is protected by the contract — a winning bet cannot return less than it cost.
    The split
    95% of the losing pool goes to the winning side, in proportion to each bet. The other 5% is the protocol fee, taken from the losing pool only.
    The fighters
    Winner takes 1.5× their stake, loser gets half of theirs back, and the winning prompt is minted as a fully on‑chain SVG. Neither may bet on themselves.

    05On-chain

    Winners go
    on-chain.

    A fight comes down to two prompts and a seed. That is small enough to store, and it means anyone holding those three values can re-run the bout and get the same ending.

    So the winner mints on Monad with all three recorded on the token, plus the stats and how the round finished. Re-run it with the same seed a year later and it ends the same way.
